# مقود (Miqwad): UAE driving-licence trainer for Dubai + Sharjah

Shared brief for every agent working on this project. Read it fully before starting.

## 1. What we are building

A single self-contained HTML page (Arabic, RTL, dark theme) that trains ONE learner:
a complete beginner, automatic car, Arabic speaker, planning to start the licence process in
about two months, in **Dubai** (RTA) or **Sharjah** (Sharjah Police / MOI). It must prepare him for:

1. The theory test (people in Sharjah call it "اختبار الإشارات"): Dubai RTA format and Sharjah format.
2. The yard / parking test (اختبار الساحة / الباركنج).
3. The road test (اختبار الطريق).

Goal: on exam day he recognises almost every sign, knows the rules, and has "been through" most
driving situations many times inside the game. Content is ordered from easiest to hardest.

Runs on iPhone Safari and laptop Chrome. Must work fully offline as a local file (zero network
requests: no CDN, no Google Fonts, no remote images). It is also published as a Claude artifact.
Progress lives only on his device (localStorage wrapped in try/catch, in-memory fallback, plus an
export/import code). It is a private learning tool, not an official product.

## 3. Accuracy rules (the most important section)

- The UAE drives on the **RIGHT**. Overtaking is on the **LEFT**. Roundabouts circulate
  **anticlockwise** (seen from above); a driver entering gives way to traffic already on the
  roundabout, which comes from the **LEFT**. Never import rules from left-hand-traffic countries
  (UK, India, Pakistan, Australia): many "UAE practice" sites copy UK text. If a fact smells like
  the UK Highway Code, verify it against a UAE source or drop it.
- New federal traffic law: **Federal Decree-Law No. 14 of 2024** on traffic regulation, in force
  **29 March 2025**, replaced Federal Law 21/1995 (minimum licence age now 17). Prefer 2025 to 2026
  sources. Old material may still be right, but check it.
- Source priority: (1) official: rta.ae, dubaipolice.gov.ae, moi.gov.ae, u.ae, shjpolice.gov.ae,
  sharjah.ae, uaelegislation.gov.ae, wam.ae, mediaoffice.ae; (2) licensed driving institutes:
  Emirates Driving Institute, Belhasa, Galadari, Dubai Driving Center, Al Ahli, Excellence,
  Sharjah Driving Institute (sdi.ae), other Sharjah institutes; (3) major newspapers (Gulf News,
  Khaleej Times, The National, Al Khaleej, Al Bayan, Emarat Al Youm); (4) practice-test sites:
  hints only, never the sole source of a fact.
- Keep a source list in your research notes with ids S1, S2, ... (title, URL, publisher, date).
  Every content item cites `src` ids and `confidence`: `"high"` (official/institute source, or two
  solid independent sources agree) or `"medium"` (one decent source). Anything weaker stays out of
  the JSON and goes to an "Unverified" list in your notes.
- When sources conflict (for example exam question counts), record both with dates, say which you
  used and why, and put a short Arabic note in the item's `notes` field. Do not guess.
- WebFetch cannot read some sites (JS-heavy or blocked). Try another page, a PDF, a search snippet,
  or another source. Do not loop on one site. The shell has no general internet access.

## 4. Arabic writing rules (every user-facing string)

- Questions and options: clear Modern Standard Arabic close to real exam wording (فصحى مبسطة).
- `explain`: short, simple Arabic, 1 to 3 short sentences. Optional `tip`: a friendly memory trick,
  may be in Levantine dialect (شامي).
- **No tanwin and no shadda anywhere**: never use the characters ً ٌ ٍ ّ (write مثلا not مثلاً,
  أيضا not أيضاً, السيارة not السيّارة).
- **No em dash (—) and no en dash (–)** as punctuation. Use "،" or ":" or parentheses.
- **No string ends with a full stop** ("." or Arabic "۔"). Question marks at the end of questions are
  fine. Inside a string, separate sentences with "،" or a line break "\n".
- Prefer active phrasing: ممنوع / مسموح / لازم / عليك / القانون يمنع. Avoid passive verb forms like
  يُمنع، يُسمح، يُعاقب، يُحظر.
- Western digits with Arabic units: "80 كم/ساعة"، "5 أمتار"، "400 درهم"، "4 نقاط سوداء".
- Left/right always mean the real-world driver's left/right.
- One unambiguous correct answer. Plausible distractors that are clearly wrong under UAE rules.
  Randomise the position of the correct option. No "كل ما سبق" / "لا شيء مما سبق".
- Write original questions; never copy practice-site wording.

## 5. Content schemas

### 5.1 Question bank: `content/q_<name>.json`
```json
{
  "module": "<name>",
  "updated": "2026-09-29",
  "sources": { "S1": {"title": "...", "url": "https://...", "publisher": "...", "date": "2025-04"} },
  "cards": [
    { "id": "<name>-card-001", "topic": "speed", "level": 1,
      "title": "عنوان قصير", "body": "شرح قصير واضح", "key": "80 كم/ساعة",
      "src": ["S1"], "confidence": "high" }
  ],
  "questions": [
    { "id": "<name>-speed-001", "topic": "speed", "level": 1, "exam": ["dubai", "sharjah"],
      "q": "...؟", "options": ["...", "...", "...", "..."], "answer": 2,
      "explain": "...", "tip": "...", "src": ["S1"], "confidence": "high", "notes": "" }
  ]
}
```
- `cards` are the teaching notes shown in lessons before practice (5 to 15 per topic, each one idea;
  `key` is an optional short highlighted fact such as a number).
- `tip` and `notes` are optional. `exam` lists where the item matters (usually both).
- Topic keys: `signs-basics, lights, markings, police, speed, distance, lanes, overtaking, priority,
  roundabouts, turning, highway, pedestrians, school-bus, emergency-vehicles, vulnerable, parking,
  lights-horn, weather, night, hazard, emergency, accidents, vehicle, law, penalties, driver,
  roadtest, yard, dubai-specific, sharjah-specific`.
- Levels: 1 أساسي (first-day basics) · 2 سهل (everyday rules, common signs) · 3 متوسط (needs thought:
  roundabout lanes, unmarked junctions, stopping distance) · 4 صعب (exceptions, combined situations,
  bad weather, emergencies) · 5 متقدم (tricky, rare, easily confused, exact numbers).

### 5.2 Sign catalog: `content/signs.json`
```json
{ "updated": "...", "sources": {...},
  "categories": [ {"key": "warning", "name": "إشارات التحذير", "desc": "..."} ],
  "signs": [
    { "id": "w-bend-right", "cat": "warning", "name": "منعطف خطر إلى اليمين",
      "meaning": "...", "action": "...", "level": 2, "confuse": ["w-bend-left", "w-double-bend-right"],
      "shape": "triangle", "colors": "white background, red border, black symbol",
      "text": "", "draw": "precise pictogram description for an SVG artist",
      "uae_note": "", "src": ["S1"], "confidence": "high" } ] }
```
ID prefixes: `w-` warning, `p-` priority (stop, give way, priority road, oncoming priority),
`r-` prohibitory and restrictive (no entry, no turns, no overtaking, limits, no parking),
`m-` mandatory, `i-` information and regulatory info (one way, dead end, lanes, crossings, parking),
`s-` services, `g-` guide and direction, `t-` temporary road works, `x-` supplementary plates.
Slugs are English kebab-case.

### 5.3 Markings, lights and hand signals: `content/markings.json`
Same shape as signs (`items` instead of `signs`), categories such as `line, transverse, crossing,
arrow, area, kerb, text, device, light, lane-signal, ped-light, police, emergency`. ID prefixes:
`mk-` markings, `kb-` kerbs, `tl-` traffic lights, `ls-` lane control signals, `pl-` pedestrian
lights, `po-` police hand signals, `ev-` emergency vehicles and school bus signals.

### 5.4 Exams and practical guides: `content/exams.json` (see the exams agent prompt)
### 5.5 Scenarios: `content/scenarios_<name>.json` (see docs/scenes.md once it exists)

## 6. Module interfaces (plain browser JS, classic scripts, no modules, no frameworks, no network)

Script order in the built page: data.js, signs/kit.js, signs/glyphs.js, other signs/*.js,
scenes.js, yard.js, app.js. Every module attaches to `window` and must not throw if another
module is missing.

- `window.DATA` = `{ signs, markings, exams, curriculum, banks: {<name>: bank}, scenarios: [...] }`
- `window.SignKit`: base shapes and helpers (read the header of src/signs/kit.js).
- `window.Signs.render(id, opts) -> SVG string`, `Signs.has(id)`, `Signs.register(id, fn)`,
  `Signs.list()`. Unknown ids render a neutral placeholder, never throw.
- `window.Scenes.mount(el, spec, opts) -> controller` (defined by the scene agent in docs/scenes.md).
- `window.Yard.mount(el, opts) -> controller`, `Yard.exercises` (defined by the yard agent in docs/yard.md).

SVG rules for anything drawn: no `id` attributes and no `<defs>` inside sign SVGs (many copies share
a page), explicit fill on every shape, text uses
`font-family="Alexandria,'Readex Pro','Space Grotesk',sans-serif"`.
