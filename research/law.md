## 1. The 2024 law: what changed (S1 unless noted)

- Federal Decree-Law 14/2024 issued 2024-09-30, in force 180 days after publication: **2025-03-29**. Repeals
  Federal Law 21/1995 (art. 51). Covers drivers, pedestrians, personal mobility users, self-driving vehicles.
- **Minimum licence age 17** (art. 10) plus medical fitness and a passed driving test; licence types, ages and
  learner rules left to the executive regulations. Learning to drive only with a permit from the licensing
  authority (art. 14).
- **Status of executive regulations**: MOI promised them within 6 months (S8), but on 2026-09-16 an FNC committee
  report said they had **not been issued** (S9). Art. 49 keeps earlier decisions in force where not contrary,
  so the **MR 178/2017 fines and points schedule (as amended) is still the one applied** (S13 cites it in June
  2026; police statements in 2025 and 2026 use its figures). Art. 45: a future Cabinet resolution will set
  administrative violations and penalties. Expect a new table at some point.
- Practical age: institutes still apply 17.5 years to start lessons and 18 for the licence pending the
  regulations (S46, June 2025; exams agent found the same on RTA and EDI pages). Recorded as a note on the
  age question.
- Crimes and penalties (Arabic text, figures exact):

| art. | act | penalty |
|---|---|---|
| 35(1) | driving under the influence of alcohol (no threshold stated) | jail and/or AED 20,000 to 100,000; licence suspended at least 3 months (1st), 6 months (2nd), cancelled (3rd) |
| 35(2) | driving under narcotics or psychotropic substances | jail and/or AED 30,000 to 200,000; suspension 6 months, 1 year, cancellation |
| 36 | driving with a suspended licence | jail up to 3 months and/or at least AED 10,000 |
| 37(1) | driving on an unrecognised foreign licence | AED 2,000 to 10,000 (1st); repeat: jail at least 3 months and/or AED 5,000 to 50,000 |
| 37(2) | driving without a licence or with a licence not valid for that vehicle type | jail up to 3 months and/or AED 5,000 to 50,000; repeat: jail at least 3 months and/or AED 20,000 to 100,000 |
| 38 | not stopping after an accident with injuries; owner withholding information; fleeing police or causing a chase; deliberately hitting police/military/security vehicles | jail up to 1 year and/or AED 50,000 to 100,000 |
| 39(1) | crossing outside designated places when it causes an accident | jail and/or AED 5,000 to 10,000 |
| 39(2) | crossing outside designated places on roads with a limit of 80 km/h or more | jail at least 3 months and/or at least AED 10,000 |
| 40 | causing death | jail and/or at least AED 50,000; at least 1 year and at least AED 100,000 if red light, DUI, suspended or cancelled licence, or driving in a flowing valley |
| 41 | refusing to give name/address or giving false data | jail up to 3 months and/or AED 10,000 to 20,000 |
| 42 (S2) | court measures | suspend licence up to 3 years, bar renewal up to 2 more years, bar obtaining one up to 3 years |
| 34 | forging, altering or transferring plates | jail and/or at least AED 20,000 |

- Driver duties (art. 5): show licence and vehicle data on request; first aid only if you have the medical
  knowledge; **report any accident caused by or to you within 3 hours** (old executive regulation art. 12 said
  6 hours); slow down at school and hospital entrances, in poor visibility, near personal mobility users, at
  crossings, and stop for pedestrians who have started crossing; no horn inside cities except to prevent danger
  or accidents; no vehicle making severe noise; obey authorities in emergencies, rain and flowing valleys; no
  random stopping that blocks traffic. Art. 7: no stopping on the road or pavement to repair a vehicle;
  pedestrians and personal mobility users may not stand in the carriageway or cross outside designated places;
  no throwing objects on the road.
- Priority order (art. 6): official convoys, civil defence, ambulances, military convoys, police using sirens and
  lights, essential service vehicles. Art. 8: licence must be valid for the vehicle type; the person
  responsible for a vehicle must not let an unlicensed person drive it. Art. 12: licence can be suspended or
  cancelled for medical unfitness. Art. 19: insurance required to register or renew. Art. 26 (via S12): no major
  modification (body, engine power, colour) without approval. Art. 28: no repair of accident damage without a
  repair permit from the traffic authority. Art. 31: arrest cases. Art. 32: impound cases (unfit or unplated
  vehicle; driving unlicensed twice, held until licence shown; unlicensed driver; unapproved modifications;
  accident evidence). Art. 43: self-driving vehicles by Cabinet decision.

## 2. Black points (S3 arts. 2 to 5, S15, S14, S16 to S19)

- Threshold **24 points within one year** from the first violation.
- Normal licence: 1st time licence withheld and suspended **3 months**, 2nd time **6 months**, 3rd time **1 year**
  (MOI adds a mandatory course).
- **Probationary licence (تحت التجربة)**: at 24 points, **6 months** from the first time; repeat: licence
  **cancelled**, no new application for **1 year**. Abu Dhabi Police (S16, S17, Oct 2025) describe the
  probationary holders as under 21 and offer a paid rehabilitation course (AED 2,400) instead of suspension.
- Points erased after a year if the threshold was not reached (MOI: two conditions). The resolution text read
  by the fetch tool counts 12 months from payment; MOI press wording says from the violation. JSON says "after a
  year" without the start point.
- Course: **8 points removed, once a year, only before reaching 24**. Fees: Abu Dhabi Police AED 800 (8 to 23
  points) and AED 2,400 to restore a licence (S18, Apr 2026); SDI AED 550 general, AED 850 special programme for
  those who reached the limit (S19). Fees kept out of the JSON.

### Sharjah practices

- Sharjah Executive Council, 2025-07-08 (S27, S28): **35%** off if paid within 60 days (covers the fine, impound
  period, storage and late fees); **25%** off the fine only between 60 days and one year; **serious violations
  excluded** (GN lists red light, phone, excessive speeding, sudden stopping, truck and pedestrian-safety
  offences). Pedestrian violations tracked through Emirates ID; plainclothes and uniformed patrols (S28).
- Minor accidents reported through **Rafid** (app or toll-free **80092**, launched with Sharjah Police
  2024-05-29; old number 80072343 still works) (S32, S34, S40).
- National Day 2025: Sharjah Police cancelled traffic points for violations before 2025-12-01 if paid
  2025-12-01 to 2026-01-10; payment through the Sharjah Police website or the MOI app (S29). One-off offer: not
  used as a rule.
- SDI point programmes: 8 points off for drivers with 23 or fewer, once a year; special programme after the
  limit (S19).

## 4. Accidents

Legal base: new law art. 5 (report within 3 hours, first aid if competent), art. 38 (not stopping when injured
persons), art. 28 (repair permit); old executive regulation art. 12 (still applicable where not contrary):
in injury accidents stop at once without creating new danger, secure traffic, do not change the scene, care for
the injured; in non-injury accidents park the vehicles in the nearest place that does not obstruct traffic;
all parties give personal and vehicle data. Art. 64: a vehicle stopped at night on an unlit road or in poor
visibility must show position lights or a reflective triangle.
